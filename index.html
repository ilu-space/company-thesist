Most university research
never leaves the lab.

Universities produce enormous volumes of publishable, fundable, and technically breakthrough research every year. But the path from academic output to commercial product is long, uncertain, and resource-intensive — and most institutions lack the dedicated capacity to walk it.


The result: commercially viable discoveries are shelved, IP sits underutilised, and researchers never learn whether their work could have changed an industry.


Thesist exists to change that.

Tech transfer offices are stretched thin. Most manage hundreds of disclosures with small teams, making deep commercial analysis of every output nearly impossible.

The gap between "publishable" and "investable" is vast. Researchers are trained to produce knowledge — not market-ready products. Bridging that gap requires a different skill set entirely.

Real market validation is rare. Most commercialisation efforts stop at desk research. Thesist goes further — testing real demand with real buyers before asking for investment.

Our Approach

A studio process built for academic innovation

We've designed a structured commercialisation sprint that takes research from raw output to investor-ready concept — in weeks, not years.

🔍
Phase 01 — Discovery

Scan your entire research portfolio

We ingest your university's publications, patent filings, grant outputs, and researcher profiles. Our AI-powered scanner evaluates each output across market fit, timing, IP defensibility, and commercial feasibility — ranking the highest-potential opportunities so you know exactly where to focus first.

AI-powered scanning Patent analysis Feasibility ranking
📊
Phase 02 — Analysis

Build a Commercial Potential Report

For each prioritised opportunity, we produce a detailed commercial map: which industries it fits, who the likely buyers are, what the addressable market looks like, what competitive alternatives exist, and what the go-to-market path could be. This report becomes the foundation for every decision that follows.

Industry mapping Market sizing Buyer personas
⚡
Phase 03 — Build

Translate research into a tangible prototype

A written report can only take you so far. We build a functional prototype or MVP — a real artefact that stakeholders, potential customers, and investors can interact with. This moves the conversation from "interesting paper" to "real product" in a matter of weeks.

Rapid prototyping MVP development Weeks, not months
✅
Phase 04 — Validate

Test demand with real buyers

We run structured outreach to potential customers, pilot partners, and industry buyers — measuring genuine willingness to pay, not just sentiment. You get real market signals: letters of intent, pilot expressions, pricing data, and objections. No assumptions. No guesswork.

Buyer outreach Demand signals Pilot conversations
🚀
Phase 05 — Launch Readiness

Hand off an investor-ready package

At the end of the sprint, you receive everything needed for the next step: a pitch deck, IP strategy recommendations, go-to-market plan, and warm introductions to relevant investors and accelerators. The research isn't just validated — it's ready to raise.
